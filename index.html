No engineer required.

Unbox the arm. Point the arm. Tell it what to do. Done — simple as that.

Current robotic arms are built for industrial factories — expensive, and useless without an engineer to program them. Munchkin Robotics is building arms for small shops instead: low cost, and easy enough that anyone on shift can put it to work.

How it works

Brief it like you'd brief a new hire.

Speak

Point the arm at a station and just tell it the task — the same way you'd brief a new hire on shift.

Watch

Onboard vision reads the station, and the arm plans the motion itself — no teach-pendant, no line of code.

Done

It runs the shift. Routine cases just happen; anything genuinely unfamiliar pages you instead of guessing.
